Metals: Cut, Bevel, Blast, Weld
PERIOD 5 · 11:50-1:20 · 90 MIN  |  PERIOD 7 · 1:30-3:00 · 90 MIN

OBJECTIVE

By the end of class you can cut, bevel, blast and tack a butt joint that sits flat and straight before you weld it.

JOURNAL

Monday was the angle grinder, one bevel on scrap. Today that bevel goes on a real joint. If you could weld one thing this year for yourself or someone else, what would it be and who is it for?

TODAY

  1. Journal, then phones in the caddy (5 min)
  2. Demo on 1 inch bar: cut, bevel, blast, tack, weld. The four joints video (in Canvas) (15 min)
  3. Short a test: retake it now, at a Chromebook. Everyone else: bandsaw two 6 inch pieces, measured twice (20 min)
  4. Bevel the edges that meet, face shield down, sparks away. Then sandblast to bright metal, lid shut (20 min)

  6. Cleanup: gas off, guns hung, pieces on your team shelf, exit ticket (10 min)

DUE

The test comes before the machine, at 100 percent: Metal Bandsaw for the saw, Portable Angle Grinder for the grinder, and contract, General Shop Safety, Arc and MIG Welding for a gun. Corrections sheet in the basket for any first try under 100.
