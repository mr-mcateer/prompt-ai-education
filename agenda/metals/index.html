THURSDAY 9/3/26 (ODD)

General Shop Safety

PERIOD 5 · 11:50-1:20 · 90 MIN  |  PERIOD 7 · 1:30-3:00 · 90 MIN

Journal

On the tour yesterday you saw the whole shop at once. Which machine would you least want to stand next to if the person running it did not know what they were doing, and why that one?

Heads up

Nobody touches a machine until General Shop Safety is passed at 100 percent. Retakes are unlimited and there is no penalty. If you miss twice, come see me and we fix it together before the third try.

Today

  1. Journal, then phones in the caddy (10 min)
  2. Glasses issued and labeled. Safety Trinity: glasses on, rings off, sleeves rolled up (15 min)
  3. Floor walk: extinguishers, E-stops, exits, eye wash, where the drops go. You point at yours from where you stand (20 min)
  4. One lap on the five machines this test covers: portable drill, drill press, band saw, cold saw, blast cabinet (20 min)
  5. General Shop Safety test on the Chromebooks (20 min)
  6. Cleanup and exit ticket (5 min)

Bring tomorrow

Closed-toe shoes. You do not get on the floor without them.

Coming up

Angle grinder next Tuesday, stick welding Thursday. Each one is a demo, then a test, then you run it once in front of me. The shop opens up one machine at a time, not all at once.
