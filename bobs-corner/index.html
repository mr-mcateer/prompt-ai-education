Bob's Corner

Bob Musil is a retired machinist. He is 84, and he gives our shop 10 to 20 hours every week.

He also sends Mr. McAteer a steady stream of shop videos. These are the best of them, sorted by topic, with what to look for and how each one connects to your class.

If one makes you curious, find Bob at his bench under the sign and ask him how he would do it.

Before you watch

  • These are other people's videos. Some shops do things ours does not allow. Our rules win every time.
  • When a card says Bob runs this kind of machine, you watch and ask. You do not run it.
  • Sleeves down and glasses on. There is no oxy-acetylene in our shop.
  • Tap a video to play it. The small icon after each credit opens the original post.
