Bob's Corner

Bob Musil is 84 and a retired machinist. He volunteers in our shop 10 to 20 hours a week.

He also sends Mr. McAteer a steady stream of shop videos. These are the best of them, sorted by topic, with what to watch for and how each one connects to your class.

If one of these makes you curious, find Bob at his bench under the sign. Ask him how he would do it.

Before you watch

  • These are other people's videos. Some shops do things our shop does not allow. Our rules win every time.
  • Machine tagged 'Bob runs this' means watch and ask. You do not run it.
  • Sleeves down, glasses on, no oxy-acetylene in our shop.
  • Videos play when you are signed in with your school Google account.
