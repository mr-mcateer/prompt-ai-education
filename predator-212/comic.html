Test 0Nobody pulls until the gate is checked

  • Glasses on. Gas can, rags and starting fluid off the bench.
  • Engine level. Oil at the lower edge of the fill hole, not thin, no gas smell.
  • Air filter off: throttle plate stands open, lever snaps back.
  • Kill switch on Run, both wires plugged in. Fuel valve off.
  • Engine number on tape. Camera ready.
  • Stand beside the recoil. Kickback means stop.
  • Coach initials the sheet. Alone at home: tick all six on paper first.

Listen firstThe engine already talks

Write what you hear on the sheet before any tool comes out. Plug wet and gassy: flooded (9). Plug bone dry: no fuel (5, 6, 10). Cord like a wall: exhaust lash too loose, release can't work (7).

Test 1Spark

Wires on first. Three pulls. Does the lamp flash every time?

Coach

YES 3 of 3Spark is fine. Go to Test 2.
SOMEWeak. Re-gap the coil, 0.010 to 0.016 in (cause 4), then test again.
NONETest 1b: unplug the yellow sensor wire at its bullet connector, pull 3. Flashes now? Cause 2. Still none: unplug the black kill wire, pull 3. Still none: coil gap (4), then key (3). Plug both wires back in.

Test 2Fuel

The fluid does the choke's job. Choke plus fluid floods it. One squirt, three pulls, twice at most.

Coach

VROOM... diesRuns a second or more, or fires in the muffler, then quits. Spark and compression are fine. It is fuel: carb (5), governor (6), or bad gas (10). Skip Test 3.
PFFTA sneeze back out the carb is not a pass. Write it down: cam timing (1) or intake lash (7).
NOTHINGTwo tries, nothing. Go to Test 3.
6

Test 3Compression

Switch off. Fuel off. Plug out, grounded on the head, away from the hole. Choke and throttle wide open. Six sharp pulls. Photograph the needle.

Coach

There is no magic number. The compression release holds a valve open while you crank, so every pull-start reading is low. Compare to a good engine on this gauge.

Coach, quietly

NORMALWithin about 15 psi of the good engine, but it would not fire on fluid: cam timing (1), then flywheel key (3). A sheared key still flashes the tester, just at the wrong moment.
LOW30 or more below. Teaspoon of oil in the hole, six more pulls. Rises 20 psi or more: rings (8). Rises under 10: valve, lash, or head gasket (7, 8). In between: do both again, lash first.
